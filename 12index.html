Backup and restore

Back up all customers, credit transactions and payment history into one file. Keep a copy somewhere safe, such as a USB drive or another device.

Restoring a backup replaces the records currently stored in this browser. Export a backup of the current records first if you need to keep them.

How offline storage works

  • Records are stored in this browser on this device.
  • Clearing browser data or uninstalling/resetting the browser may erase records.
  • Opening the app on another device does not automatically bring your records across.
  • Use the backup file to move or restore your records.
